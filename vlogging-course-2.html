Your life isn't boring. You just need to know how to tell the story.

A lot of people want to vlog.

They record their day. They collect footage. They edit it. They post it.

And then wonder why nobody seems interested.

The problem usually isn't that their life is boring.

It's that they haven't learned what to film, what to leave out, how to structure the story, or how to turn ordinary moments into something people actually want to watch.

Vlogging isn't simply recording your life. It's deciding what parts of your life are worth showing and shaping them into a story.

That's what this course teaches.

You'll learn the process I've developed through years of making vlogs — from figuring out what to film to turning the footage into a story.

What you'll learn

Six weeks. Six modules. A practical system for making better vlogs.

01Foundations

Understand what makes a vlog work.

Learn what separates an engaging vlog from a collection of random clips, and develop the mindset you need to start creating consistently.

02Pre-Vlog

Know what to vlog and find the story before you start filming.

Learn how to find stories in your everyday life and decide what is actually worth capturing.

03Shooting

Capture the footage you actually need.

Learn how to shoot your vlogs intentionally, including the shots, sequences and techniques that make editing easier later.

You can start with the equipment you already have.

04Editing

Turn your footage into a story people want to finish.

Learn how to select, arrange and edit your footage so that your vlog has structure, momentum and a reason to keep watching.

05Voiceover

Use your voice to connect the story.

Learn how to use voiceover to add context, personality and narrative to your footage.

06Posting & Growth

Publish your vlogs strategically and learn from what happens next.

Learn how to package and post your vlogs, understand your audience and use what you learn from each video to make the next one better.
