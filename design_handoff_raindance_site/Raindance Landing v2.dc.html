THE STORY

The Rainmaker has declared war on Nigeria's political elite, kidnapping the Senate President's daughter in his latest act. The lawmaker hires Ali—a broken ex-superhero who gave up his powers after devastating losses.

Desperate to save his terminally ill grandfather, Ali accepts the job. Neither man knows it, but their collision is the latest move in a war for the soul of Nigeria five hundred years in the making.

"Citizens of the hopeless Republic of Nigeria… Today, I bring you fire."

THE AUTHOR

Gilbert Bassey

Gilbert Bassey is a Nigerian storyteller who discovered the freedom of novel writing after earning his engineering degree. He is the creator of A Tale of Gods and Men, a saga blending Nigerian history, politics, fantasy and mythology. His stories marry spectacle and substance, explore the tension between light and darkness, and grapple with the moral complexities of being human.

He is also the author of A Decent Man. Based in Lagos, he creates music and content as Gillian Baci.
