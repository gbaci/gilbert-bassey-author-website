a new novel, writing updates, and an overdue invitation

ATOGAM is coming. here is where things stand.

By Gilbert Bassey July 8, 2026 3 min read

a lot has happened since i released A Decent Man earlier this year, and i have been meaning to write to you about it.

the biggest thing

my next novel, Ali: Raindance, is going to be released in January 2027.

it is the first of six books in the A Tale of Gods and Men (ATOGAM) saga — a fantasy epic that blends political realism, African mythology, superhero vibes, and the mechanics of institutional transformation.

we just finished getting feedback from beta readers and i have been genuinely moved by what they sent back. special shoutout to the thirty-five people who read and filled the form. you helped shape something i have been building for years.

i have also been writing the first draft of Book 2 (currently in chapter 50 of 62). i aim to finish this within the next 2-3 weeks and then it is full steam ahead for Ali: Raindance. more about it as we get closer.

now, the overdue part

for the last few weeks, i have been posting Lightbringer letters on Substack instead of here because it was easier to work from there. i should have told you sooner. i am sorry i did not.

here is what you have been missing:

the letter i wrote myself — on writing yourself encouragement not to fix what is broken, but to close the distance between paralysed and capable.

how to lose gracefully — Saul had a kingdom, an army, everything. then David rose. and instead of letting the loss be what it was, Saul spent the rest of his life fighting a war that was already over. this letter is about what it costs when we refuse to let a loss be done.

the downbeat cycle — those stretches where everything feels wrong and your mind builds a whole case against you using your real flaws as evidence. the insight is not to argue back. it is to recognise the cycle so you do not make permanent decisions inside a temporary feeling.

a new letter goes out every Friday. if this is the kind of thinking you want in your inbox, come subscribe to Lightbringer on Substack.

the other thing worth mentioning

i had a great time teaching the first cohort of The Storytelling Course earlier this year. we finished two weeks ago and i felt fulfilled that the students loved the course. we may open a second cohort before the year ends, though i cannot say for certain yet.

that's all for today. i will send out more of these as we get closer to release for Ali: Raindance.

Gilbert
