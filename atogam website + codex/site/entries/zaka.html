Zaka — the Rainmaker

The masked figure responsible for the campaign against the capital.

Public accounts vary and most are unreliable. Common claims — that he arrived with the rain, that the abducted children of the political class were returned altered — are unverified. One detail is consistent across sources and appears accurate: no ransom was ever demanded.

Assessment of him divides by district. In Maitama he is described as a terrorist. In Apo the description is less immediate.

He was raised at the Lighthouse and left of his own decision. Both facts stand without resolution.

⌁ Spoiler · Raindance

Reveals how the Rainmaker's story ends — his name, his death, and who he was.

⌁ Revealed · Raindance

His name was Zaka. Dalinar found him on a street in Abuja at fifteen and brought him to the Lighthouse himself. He served in the army, returned harder, and left the Lighthouse for good saying the fire could not wait any longer.

He died at twenty-six, killed by Ali. He was the last surviving member of a family of four: his mother, his sister Suzzie, and his brother Daniel, who died eleven years before him.

He believed the campaign was his own and he died believing he had failed.
