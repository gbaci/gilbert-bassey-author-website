The full story, for a longer post.

What if Nigeria’s next political crisis had a superhero in it? Would you be interested in reading about it?

Here’s the gist:

A masked terrorist known as the Rainmaker kidnaps the families of Nigeria’s most powerful politicians and turns their deaths into a national spectacle. The police can’t find him. The government can’t stop him. And so they turn to an unconventional weapon—Ali.

A burned-out ex-superhero, Ali possesses an extraordinary ability: he can feel what other people feel—their joy, their fear, their suffering. And in a country drowning in all three, it’s a gift he would rather not have. He’s spent years trying to escape that life. But when the Senate President seeks his help to find and rescue his kidnapped daughter, Ali is pulled back in.

Ali has no interest in helping politicians. But when his grandfather is hospitalized with a terminal illness, he can’t afford to turn down the job.

What follows is a battle of ideology between a man who wants nothing to do with the country and a terrorist who wants to burn it to the ground.

Neither man knows it, but their confrontation is the latest move in a war for the soul of Nigeria, 500 years in the making.

The book is scheduled for publication on 1 January 2027, and the author is giving away free digital copies.
