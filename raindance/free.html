THE STORY

A masked terrorist. A broken hero. A nation on the brink.

The Rainmaker has kidnapped the families of Nigeria's most powerful people and turned their deaths into national spectacle. The government can't find him. The police can't stop him. So they hire Ali.

A former soldier with extraordinary abilities, Ali can find almost anyone. The problem is that he can also feel what everyone around him feels, and after years of carrying other people's pain, he's done being a hero. He takes the job for one reason: the grandfather who raised him is dying, and the hospital wants money Ali doesn't have.

What begins as a rescue mission will draw Ali into something far older: a war for the soul of Nigeria, five hundred years in the making.
