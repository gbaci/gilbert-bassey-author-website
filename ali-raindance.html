The Story

The Rainmaker has come to Abuja

Ali is a depressed twenty-five-year-old Nigerian with superpowers who long abandoned his youthful superhero ways after a series of monumental losses. A new loss threatens him when his grandfather is diagnosed with a terminal illness, forcing him out of early retirement.

Around this time, and against the backdrop of election fever, chaos arrives in Abuja in the form of the Rainmaker, a brutally efficient terrorist whose main victims are top politicians and government officials.

When the Senate President’s daughter is kidnapped, he hires Ali to rescue her. Normally not one to work for politicians, desperation forces Ali to take the job, putting him on a collision course with the Rainmaker. What follows will determine the fate of both men, and Nigeria itself.

The Saga

A Tale of Gods and Men

Ali: Raindance is the first of six books in the A Tale of Gods and Men (ATOGAM) saga, a fantasy epic about the war for the soul of Nigeria. It blends political realism, African mythology, superhero spectacle, and the slow, costly work of changing a nation from the inside.

I have been building this world for over nine years. Thirty-five beta readers helped shape this first book. Now it is your turn.
